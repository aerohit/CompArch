Stanford CS149: Parallel Computing

Self-contained study pages, one per lecture of Stanford's CS149 (Fall 2023, taught by Kayvon Fatahalian and Kunle Olukotun). Each follows its lecture's argument, explains every concept from the ground up, and adds diagrams, worked examples and explorers you can manipulate.

These pages complement the main course notes. CS149 looks at hardware from a software engineer's point of view: what each architectural feature demands from your program. Where a lecture relies on background from the course notes, the page recaps it and links to the relevant unit.
